# Bulletbound
Bulletbound is an action-packed, single-level zombie survival shooter where every bullet counts. Face off against relentless zombie hordes across 5 challenging waves and unlock powerful abilities as you rack up your kill count.

Take out enemies to earn game-changing power-ups like Double Score, Faster Fire Rate, Shield Protection, High Jump, and Health Boost. The more zombies you destroy, the stronger you become — but so do they.

With tight controls, fast gameplay, and escalating difficulty, Bulletbound delivers a short but intense survival experience. Can you survive the chaos and master the battlefield?

Key Features:

🧟 Survive through 5 intense waves of zombie attacks

🔫 Unlockable power-ups: Fire rate, shield, score multiplier, jump, health

🧍 One level — pure arcade survival challenge

⚡ Fast, rewarding gameplay loop with high replay value
