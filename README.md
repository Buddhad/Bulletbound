## Bulletbound
Bulletbound is a fast-paced 2D zombie survival shooter where every bullet counts. Fight through 5 challenging levels, survive relentless zombie attacks, collect coins, and use the in-game shop to gain powerful temporary abilities that can turn the tide of battle.
As you progress, the zombie threat becomes more intense. Manage your ammunition, movement, health, and abilities carefully as you fight your way through each level and aim for the highest possible score.
Key Features
- 🧟 5 Challenging Levels
  Fight your way through five progressively challenging levels filled with zombie attacks.
- 🔫 Fast-Paced Shooting
  Use your limited ammunition wisely, reload when necessary, and survive increasingly dangerous encounters.
- 🪙 Coin-Based Shop System
  Defeat zombies and collect coins, then spend them in the shop to gain useful upgrades and supplies.
- ⚡ Powerful Abilities
  Purchase temporary abilities including:
  - 🔥 Faster Fire Rate
  - 🛡️ Shield Protection
  - ❤️ Health Boost
  - 🪙 Double Coins
  - 🔫 Additional Bullets
- 📈 Progressive Challenge
  Each level pushes your survival skills further with increasingly demanding zombie encounters.
- 🏆 Score & High Score System
  Defeat enemies, build your score, and try to beat your personal high score.
- 🔄 Replayable Arcade Gameplay
  A simple and rewarding gameplay loop designed for quick runs and repeated attempts.
- 🎮 Simple, Responsive Controls
  Move, aim, shoot, reload, collect coins, purchase abilities, and survive.
